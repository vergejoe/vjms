清晨，小猫走到窗边，看见花园里开了一朵黄色的小花。它轻轻推开门，想把这个发现告诉朋友。

小河边住着一只白兔。每天傍晚，它都带着一本书坐在树下，听风吹过叶子的声音。

今天下雨了。小熊拿起蓝色雨伞，走到路口，等没有带伞的小狐狸一起回家。

妹妹在桌上放了两个苹果。一个留给自己，一个留给放学回来的哥哥。她还准备了两杯水。

夜晚，天空中出现了许多星星。爸爸告诉孩子，观察星空需要耐心，也需要一个晴朗的晚上。

山坡上有一间小木屋。屋前种着蔬菜，屋后长着果树。春天来了，大家一起整理花园。

男孩第一次学着骑车，摔倒后有点难过。妈妈扶起自行车，陪着他慢慢练习。他终于骑到了小路尽头。

图书馆里很安静。女孩找到一本关于海洋的书，读到了鲸鱼、珊瑚和遥远的岛屿。

小狗听见门外的脚步声，立刻跑过去。原来是主人回来了，手里还带着一袋新鲜的面包。

老师让每个孩子种一颗种子。几个星期后，窗台上的小盆里都长出了绿色的嫩芽。

小船停在湖边。爷爷检查好绳子和船桨，带着孙女去看水面上的荷花。他们没有忘记穿救生衣。

冬天的早晨，院子里积了一层白雪。孩子们戴好手套，用雪堆出了一个圆圆的雪人。

小鸟在树枝上唱歌。树下的小猫抬头听了一会儿，又躺回暖和的阳光里，安静地睡着了。

朋友送来一张手写的卡片。卡片上画着一朵花，写着祝你生日快乐。她把卡片认真收进盒子里。

厨房里传来了饭菜的香味。哥哥洗好青菜，妹妹摆好碗筷，一家人准备一起吃晚饭。

花园里的向日葵长高了。每天，小女孩都会给它浇一点水，并在本子上记下它的变化。

公交车在站台停下。一位老人走上车，男孩站起身，把靠窗的座位让给了他。

假日里，家人一起去爬山。他们带着水和食物，沿着小路慢慢走，终于看到了山顶的风景。

小狐狸弄丢了自己的红围巾。朋友们陪它走过桥边和树林，最后在一张长椅上找到了围巾。

女孩在学习画画。她用蓝色画天空，用绿色画草地，又在中间画了一座有红色屋顶的小房子。

One morning, a little cat looked out of the window. A yellow flower was growing in the garden. The cat opened the door and went to tell a friend.

A white rabbit lived beside a quiet river. Every evening, she brought a book to the old tree and listened to the wind in its leaves.

It was raining when the bear left home. He carried a blue umbrella and waited at the corner for his friend, who had forgotten hers.

The girl put two apples on the table. One was for her and one was for her brother. Then she filled two cups with fresh water.

Many stars appeared in the night sky. The father told his child that watching the stars takes patience and a clear evening.

A small wooden house stood on the hill. Vegetables grew in front and fruit trees grew behind it. In spring, everyone helped in the garden.

The boy fell while learning to ride his bike. His mother helped him stand up and held the bike steady. Soon he reached the end of the path.

The library was quiet. A girl found a book about the ocean. She read about whales, coral, and islands far away from her home.

The dog heard footsteps outside the door and ran to meet its owner. There was a bag of fresh bread in his hand.

The teacher gave each child a seed. A few weeks later, green shoots appeared in the small pots along the classroom window.

The boat waited beside the lake. Grandfather checked the rope and the oars. He and his granddaughter put on life jackets before leaving.

Snow covered the yard on a winter morning. The children put on their gloves and worked together to make a round snowman.

A bird sang on a branch above the sleeping cat. The cat listened for a moment, then closed its eyes again in the warm sunshine.

Her friend sent a card with a flower drawn on it. The message wished her a happy birthday. She carefully placed the card in a little box.

The kitchen smelled of dinner. The brother washed the vegetables while his sister set the table. Soon the whole family would eat together.

The sunflower grew taller in the garden. Every day, the girl gave it some water and wrote down what had changed in her notebook.

The bus stopped and an older man stepped inside. A boy stood up and offered him the seat beside the window.

On their day off, the family climbed a hill. They carried food and water and walked slowly along the path until they reached the top.

The fox lost a red scarf. Friends helped search near the bridge and through the woods. At last, they found it on a wooden bench.

The girl was learning to paint. She used blue for the sky and green for the grass. In the middle, she painted a house with a red roof.
